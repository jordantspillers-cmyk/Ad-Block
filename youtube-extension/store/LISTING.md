# Chrome Web Store listing (copy and paste)

**Name:** TubeShield – Private YouTube Ad Blocker

**Summary (132 chars max):**
Blocks YouTube video ads, banner ads and ad tracking. No accounts, no analytics: your data stays in your browser.

**Category:** Productivity (or Tools)  ·  **Language:** English

**Description:**

TubeShield removes ads from YouTube and keeps your data on your computer.

WHAT IT BLOCKS
• Pre-roll and mid-roll video ads, removed before the player loads them
• Any ad that still slips through: muted, fast-forwarded and skipped automatically
• Home feed, search, sidebar, banner and overlay ads
• Requests to ad servers and ad-tracking beacons from YouTube pages
• The "ad blockers are not allowed" popup

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-up, no servers
• No analytics, no telemetry, no remote code
• Settings and counters are stored only in Chrome on your computer
• Export, import or erase your data any time from Settings
• Only runs on youtube.com. It can't see any other site.

SIMPLE
• One switch to pause it, separate switches for each feature
• Works on youtube.com, music.youtube.com and embedded players

**Single purpose (Privacy practices tab):**
Block advertisements and ad tracking on YouTube.

**Permission justifications (Privacy practices tab):**
- Host permission (youtube.com, youtube-nocookie.com): Needed to hide ad elements on YouTube pages and remove ad data from YouTube's player responses. The extension runs on no other sites.
- declarativeNetRequest: Blocks requests from YouTube pages to known ad and ad-tracking endpoints.
- scripting: Registers or removes the ad-removal script when the user turns that feature on or off.
- storage: Saves the user's settings and ad counters locally.

**Remote code:** No, I am not using remote code.

**Data usage:** Tick nothing. TubeShield collects no user data. Then tick the three certification boxes.
